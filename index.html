About Me

I'm a Software Engineer II at Amazon Prime Video, where I architect and scale the cloud infrastructure powering some of the biggest live sporting events on the planet. From NFL Thursday Night Football to the NBA, MLB, UEFA Champions League, ICC Cricket, and beyond — I design the modular, fault-tolerant services that keep millions of viewers streaming without a hitch, even during the most intense traffic spikes.

My day-to-day involves building distributed systems that manage concurrent processes at massive scale, designing real-time monitoring dashboards, and automating operational workflows that keep 24/7 streaming channels running smoothly. I thrive on solving complex problems where reliability, low latency, and scalability are non-negotiable.

My path to software engineering wasn't a straight line — and that's what makes it interesting. I started as a Process Engineer at Ormco Corporation, where I implemented automated product lines with vision systems and robotics. From there, I moved to Palomar Products, debugging intercommunication systems for aerospace and naval applications. That hands-on hardware experience gave me a deep appreciation for systems thinking that I carry into every service I build today.

I hold a Master's degree in Computer Science from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and a B.S. in Electro-Mechanical Engineering. Whether it's optimizing cloud infrastructure, diving into machine learning research, or mentoring fellow engineers, I'm always looking for the next challenge to tackle.
